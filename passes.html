Which pass suits me?

Start with the intro offer when you are new. Monthly 8 supports a twice-weekly routine. Monthly 12 suits three sessions per week. Private sessions provide individual attention.

What happens after choosing?

  1. Create a prototype browser account.
  2. The selected pass appears in the member area.
  3. No real payment is taken.
  4. A live version must connect to a secure payment and booking service.
