METHODOLOGY

How to interpret v1.2

Area ≠ property

Candidate scores describe strategic area-level suitability. They do not establish whether an individual building is suitable.

Radial buffer ≠ travel catchment

The 500 m, 1 km and 2 km circles measure straight-line distance. Future versions should add road-network travel-time isochrones.

Screening weights

Premium customer fit 25%
Resident + daytime demand 20%
Health / wellness ecosystem 15%
Office / hotel activity 10%
Accessibility 10%
Competition white-space 10%
Parking / environment 10%

Property-level stage

A final lease decision should add rent, floor area, vehicle parking, road width, floor level, lift/stairs, natural light, ceiling height, frontage and access.

Evidence discipline

Approximate business listings and representative neighborhood centers must not be treated as exact surveyed coordinates. Precision is disclosed in each map popup.
