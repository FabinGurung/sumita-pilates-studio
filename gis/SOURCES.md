## Score methodology
The candidate scores are **strategic screening scores created for this project**, not authoritative GIS statistics. The model uses:

- premium customer fit — 25%
- resident + daytime catchment — 20%
- health / wellness ecosystem — 15%
- offices / hotels / international activity — 10%
- accessibility — 10%
- competition white-space — 10%
- parking / site environment — 10%

A property-level decision requires real rent, parking, frontage, floor plan, natural light, travel-time and customer-demand validation.
