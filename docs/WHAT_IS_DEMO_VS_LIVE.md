# What Is Demonstration Data and What Is Live?

## Demonstration only

- Business name, address, phone and email
- Instructor names, biographies and qualifications
- Membership prices
- Class schedule and capacity
- Testimonials
- Member account, PIN and progress information
- Booking confirmation, cancellation and waitlist behaviour
- Pass purchases

## Functional in the visitor's browser

- Navigation and mobile menu
- Schedule generation and filters
- Booking/waitlist records saved in local storage
- Member account saved in local storage
- Reservation cancellation rule
- Calendar file download
- Contact email and WhatsApp links
- Owner data-file downloads

## Not a live business system

This static site has no shared database. Different customers do not see each other's reservations. Refreshing does not remove data, but clearing browser storage does. Data does not automatically return to the Excel workbook.

## Required before public launch

1. Replace every placeholder and demonstration claim.
2. Confirm business name, logo rights, address and contact information.
3. Verify all instructor qualifications and obtain photo/biography consent.
4. Approve prices, taxes, validity, cancellation, refund and freeze policies.
5. Add privacy, terms, health screening, waiver and data-deletion pages.
6. Connect a secure booking/payment provider or custom backend.
7. Test automated confirmations, reminders, waitlists and capacity locking.
8. Make the member area private and secure.
9. Remove or protect `tools/data-editor.html`.
10. Perform accessibility, security and mobile testing.
